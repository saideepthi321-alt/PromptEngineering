## Part 1: Prompt Design & Iteration
### 1.1 Scenario A: Technical Explanation

#### Goal

Explain the concept of **Neural Networks** to a 10-year-old child.

---

#### Initial Prompt (Version 1)

**Prompt:**

Explain neural networks.

#### Output Evaluation

The response was technically correct, but it used difficult words such as artificial neurons, layers, and mathematical calculations. A 10-year-old child may find it hard to understand.

---

#### Refined Prompt (Version 2)

**Prompt:**

Explain neural networks to a 10-year-old child using simple language and everyday examples. Avoid technical words.

#### Output Evaluation

The explanation became simpler and easier to understand. However, it still felt like a textbook explanation and was not very engaging for a child.

---

#### Refined Prompt (Version 3)

**Prompt:**

Explain neural networks to a 10-year-old child using a simple example from daily life. Use short sentences, easy words, and avoid technical terms. End with a short summary.

#### Output Evaluation

The explanation became more engaging and easier to understand. However, adding a story would make it even more relatable and memorable.

---

#### Final Prompt

**Prompt:**

Explain neural networks to a 10-year-old child using a simple story. Imagine a child named Rahul is learning to identify dogs and cats from pictures. Use easy language, short sentences, and everyday examples. Avoid technical words completely. Explain how Rahul improves by practising and how a neural network learns in a similar way. End with a 3-point summary.

---

#### Final LLM Response

Imagine a boy named Rahul.

Rahul wants to learn how to tell the difference between dogs and cats in pictures.

At first, Rahul makes many mistakes. Sometimes he sees a dog and says it is a cat. Sometimes he sees a cat and says it is a dog.

So Rahul starts practising.

Every day, he looks at many pictures of dogs and cats. Slowly, he begins to notice some differences.

* Dogs usually have longer noses.
* Cats often have pointy ears.
* Dogs and cats move differently.

Whenever Rahul makes a mistake, his teacher tells him the correct answer. Rahul learns from his mistakes and remembers what he learned.

After looking at hundreds of pictures, Rahul becomes very good at identifying dogs and cats.

A neural network learns in a similar way.

Instead of using a human brain, it uses a computer. It looks at many examples, makes a guess, checks if the guess is correct, and learns from its mistakes.

The more examples it sees, the better it becomes.

This is how neural networks can learn to:

* Recognise faces in photos
* Understand spoken words
* Translate languages
* Recommend videos and songs

##### Summary

1. A neural network learns by looking at many examples.
2. It improves by learning from mistakes.
3. More practice helps it make better decisions.

---

#### Reflection on Prompt Iteration

| Version | Improvement Made                                      | Result                                                |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| V1      | Basic prompt                                          | Too technical for children                            |
| V2      | Added target audience and simple language             | Easier to understand                                  |
| V3      | Added everyday example and summary                    | More engaging                                         |
| Final   | Added story, learning process, and clear instructions | Simple, relatable, and easy for a child to understand |

### 1.1 Scenario B: Professional Email

#### Goal

Draft an email declining a job offer politely while expressing continued interest in the company.

---

#### Initial Prompt (Version 1)

**Prompt:**

Write an email declining a job offer.

##### Output Evaluation

The email declined the offer, but it was too short and direct. It did not express gratitude or maintain a positive relationship with the company.

---

#### Refined Prompt (Version 2)

**Prompt:**

Write a professional email declining a job offer. Thank the company for the opportunity and be polite.

##### Output Evaluation

The email was more professional and included appreciation. However, it did not explain that the candidate would like to stay connected with the company for future opportunities.

---

#### Refined Prompt (Version 3)

**Prompt:**

Write a professional email declining a job offer. Thank the hiring team for their time and effort. Politely decline the offer because another opportunity was accepted. Express interest in staying connected and working with the company in the future.

##### Output Evaluation

The email was professional and polite. However, adding a clear subject line and a warmer closing would make it more effective.

---

#### Final Prompt

**Prompt:**

Write a professional and polite email declining a job offer after accepting another opportunity. Thank the hiring manager and interview team for their time and consideration. Clearly but respectfully decline the offer. Express genuine interest in the company and mention that you would welcome future opportunities to work together. Use a professional tone and keep the email concise.

---

#### Final LLM Response

**Subject:** Thank You for the Job Offer

Dear Hiring Manager,

Thank you very much for offering me the position at your company. I sincerely appreciate the time and effort that you and the interview team invested throughout the recruitment process.

After careful consideration, I have decided to accept another opportunity that is more aligned with my current career goals. Therefore, I must respectfully decline your offer.

This was not an easy decision, as I was impressed by the company, the team, and the exciting work being done. I truly appreciate the opportunity to learn more about your organisation during the interview process.

I hope to stay in touch and would welcome the opportunity to work together in the future if circumstances align.

Thank you once again for your understanding, and I wish you and the team continued success.

Kind regards,

[Your Name]

---

| Version | Improvement Made                                              | Result                               |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| V1      | Basic request                                                 | Too short and lacked professionalism |
| V2      | Added gratitude and polite tone                               | More professional                    |
| V3      | Added reason and future interest                              | Better relationship management       |
| Final   | Added clear structure, subject line, and professional closing | Polished and workplace-ready email   |

Create five short and memorable marketing taglines for a modern eco-friendly water bottle brand. The taglines should be catchy, positive, and inspire people to make environmentally responsible choices. Use simple language and a modern marketing style.

### 1.3 Role and Context Analysis

#### Scenario A: Technical Explanation

##### Role Assignment

"You are an experienced primary school teacher who specializes in explaining science and technology concepts to children."

##### Context Setting

"The audience is a 10-year-old child with no technical background. Explain neural networks using simple language, everyday examples, and a short story."

---

#### Scenario C: Creative Content

##### Role Assignment

"You are a professional marketing copywriter with experience creating taglines for sustainable consumer brands."

##### Context Setting

"The product is a new eco-friendly water bottle brand targeting students and young professionals. The brand image should be modern, stylish, and environmentally responsible."

---

#### Explanation

Adding role assignment helped the AI adopt the appropriate expertise and tone for each task. In Scenario A, the teacher role encouraged simple explanations and child-friendly examples. In Scenario C, the copywriter role resulted in more creative and marketable taglines. Providing context about the audience, purpose, and brand helped the AI generate outputs that were more relevant, focused, and aligned with the intended goals.


## Part 2: Temperature & Parameter Control
### 2.1 Temperature Experimentation
#### Selected Task

Writing a product description for a laptop

---

#### Base Prompt

Write a product description for a new lightweight laptop designed for students and professionals.

---

#### Test 1 - Low Temperature

##### Prompt Used

Write a product description for a new lightweight laptop designed for students and professionals. Be precise and consistent.

##### Output

This lightweight laptop is designed for students and professionals who need reliable performance for everyday tasks. It features a fast processor, long battery life, and a compact design that makes it easy to carry. The laptop is suitable for studying, office work, online meetings, and web browsing.

---

#### Test 2 - Medium Temperature

##### Prompt Used

Write a product description for a new lightweight laptop designed for students and professionals.

##### Output

Stay productive wherever you go with this lightweight laptop built for modern students and professionals. With its powerful performance, long-lasting battery, and sleek design, it easily handles assignments, presentations, video calls, and multitasking throughout the day. Its portable build makes it the perfect companion for classrooms, offices, and travel.

---

#### Test 3 - High Temperature

##### Prompt Used

Write a product description for a new lightweight laptop designed for students and professionals. Be creative and explore diverse ideas.

##### Output

Meet the laptop that moves as fast as your ambitions. Ultra-light and stylish, it slips effortlessly into your backpack while delivering the power to conquer deadlines, presentations, creative projects, and late-night study sessions. Whether you're working from a busy café, attending virtual meetings, or bringing your next big idea to life, this laptop keeps you inspired, connected, and ready for anything.

---

#### Comparison Table

| Aspect           | Low Temperature                         | Medium Temperature         | High Temperature                           |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Writing Style    | Factual and straightforward             | Balanced and engaging      | Highly creative and persuasive             |
| Creativity Level | Low                                     | Medium                     | High                                       |
| Focus            | Product features                        | Features and benefits      | Emotional appeal and storytelling          |
| Tone             | Neutral                                 | Professional               | Inspirational                              |
| Predictability   | Very predictable                        | Moderately predictable     | Less predictable                           |
| Marketing Impact | Limited                                 | Good                       | Strong                                     |
| Best Use Case    | Technical documentation, specifications | Product listings, websites | Advertising campaigns, promotional content |

---

### 2.2 Analysis & Recommendations

Low temperature should be used when accuracy, consistency, and predictability are important. Two specific use cases are generating technical documentation and answering factual customer support questions, where clear and reliable information is required.

High temperature should be used when creativity and idea generation are the main goals. Two specific use cases are writing marketing advertisements and brainstorming story ideas, where unique and engaging content is preferred.

For the laptop product description task, the medium temperature (0.7) produced the best result. It provided a good balance between factual information and marketing appeal. The description remained clear and professional while also being engaging enough to attract potential customers.
